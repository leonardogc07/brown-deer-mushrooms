About Our Farm

Welcome to Brown Deer Mushrooms, your local source for premium, freshly harvested gourmet mushrooms. We specialize in cultivating high-quality specialty mushrooms using sustainable farming practices.


Our passion for mycology drives us to deliver the finest flavors directly to your table. Each mushroom is hand-picked at peak freshness and delivered to ensure maximum flavor and nutritional value.


Located in the heart of Winston Salem, NC, we're committed to supporting local food systems while providing exceptional culinary ingredients for home cooks and professional chefs alike.

Our Gourmet Varieties

Chestnut

Meaty texture with rich, umami flavor. Perfect for stir-fries, soups, and grilling.

Oyster

Delicate flavor with velvety texture. Great for pasta, risotto, and sautéing.

Lion's Mane

Seafood-like flavor, unique appearance. Excellent for seafood substitutes.

Enoki

True culinary delicacy, celebrated for their delicate texture and nuanced flavor profile.
