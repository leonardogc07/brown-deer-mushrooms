Our Farm in Winston-Salem

Brown Deer Mushrooms is a small gourmet mushroom farm in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. We grow specialty mushrooms with sustainable methods and harvest them by hand for home cooks and local chefs.

What we grow

  • Chestnut: meaty texture and a nutty flavor
  • Oyster: soft and ready for a quick sauté
  • Lion's Mane: mild, with a seafood-like taste. See the cooking guide.
  • Golden Enoki: slender clusters with a light bite

Find ideas for each variety on our recipes page.
