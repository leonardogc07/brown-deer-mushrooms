How we run the farm

Running several batches and varieties at once is where small mushroom farms get stressed. Every strain has its own timings, every room has limited space, and maintenance never stops. We built Kanto, a mushroom farm management system, to manage our own production at Brown Deer Mushrooms, and we use it to keep every batch and variety on track, from inoculation to harvest.

How it works

You set the harvest date, and Kanto works backward to schedule every grow step from your variety recipes, then puts batch work and farm maintenance in one Production Calendar view.

What we use it for

  1. Harvest-first planning.
  2. Varieties, recipes, and task templates.
  3. Production Calendar with batch work and maintenance on one grid, filterable by variety.
  4. Batch Planner for stacking multiple batches.
  5. Fruiting room capacity.
  6. Materials Plan and supply alerts.
  7. Equipment Planner for sterilizer runs.
  8. Orders and invoices.
  9. Access for a small team (one farm, up to 3 users).

It runs in your web browser.
