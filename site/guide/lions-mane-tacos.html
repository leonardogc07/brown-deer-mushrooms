Lion's mane

Lion's Mane Tacos

One lion's mane makes about 12 tacos. We shred it by hand, cook it until it browns, then add oil, onions, and peppers.

  • Yield about 12 tacos

We grow this lion's mane on our farm in Winston-Salem, and you can find it at our Saturday stand (7 AM to noon, Winston-Salem Fairgrounds Farmers Market). See our contact page.

Ingredients

  • 1 Lion's Mane (makes about 12 tacos)
  • Oil
  • Onion
  • Yellow pepper
  • Green pepper
  • Mexican spice blend (garlic, cumin, dried onion, paprika, and similar)
  • Flour tortillas or corn tortillas
  • Corn
  • Tomato
  • Mayo
  • Sriracha
  • A little apple cider vinegar

On the plate

We prefer flour tortillas, but flour or corn both work. On top of the filling we put corn, tomato, and a creamy sauce. The sauce is mayo mixed with sriracha and a little apple cider vinegar.

Steps

  1. We shred the lion's mane by hand.

  2. We cook the lion's mane first, until it gets color and browns.

  3. Then we add oil, onions, and peppers and cook until they are soft.

  4. We season with a Mexican spice blend. Ours has garlic, cumin, dried onion, paprika, and similar.

  5. We fill the tortillas and top them with corn, tomato, and a creamy sauce. We prefer flour tortillas, but flour or corn both work. The sauce is mayo mixed with sriracha and a little apple cider vinegar.
