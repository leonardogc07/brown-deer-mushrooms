Questions

How long until harvest?

Mushroom Grow Kit Co. says most oyster kits are ready for a first harvest in about 7 to 10 days after you start them. They say gold oyster takes longer, closer to 10 to 14 days, and lion's mane usually takes 2 to 3 weeks. Their instruction pages also list slightly different windows by variety, so follow the sheet that comes with the kit.

Where do the kits ship from?

Their shipping policy lists a Scottsdale, Arizona address and says orders normally ship within 2 to 3 business days. Their product pages say shipping is free on orders of $125 and up, to the contiguous United States.
